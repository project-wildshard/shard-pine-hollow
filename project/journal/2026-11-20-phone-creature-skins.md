# Phone skins for Pine's creatures

> Written by: Claude

ONE-FORMAT OF23f. Pine's species looks have variant tables, and until now a
variant look had no phone skin, so a phone loaded the desktop creature maps (the
untiered stopgap OF23a shipped with). Every hull look now bakes a phone tier from
the hull's phone rig, phone maps and phone coats, and the phone fetches none of
the desktop ones.

Pinned (opus-pine9): engine 0.1.58 from npm, SDK 0.4.21 and format 0.63.0,
with OF23e2 (no day host) and OF23e3 (the weather row) on the same branch.
The CI21 candidate moves to the v0.1.58 release commit. Checkpoints are the
same digests as on 0.1.57.
